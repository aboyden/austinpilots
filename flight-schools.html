Austin-Bergstrom (KAUS) & Austin Executive (EDC)

Above and Beyond Aviation

One of Austin's longest-running flight schools (est. 2000s), a Cessna Pilot Center offering Private, Instrument, Commercial, and CFI training plus aircraft rental, based at KAUS with a second location at Austin Executive Airport.

Central Texas' only Cirrus Training Partner, based at Georgetown Executive Airport. Part 141 and Part 61 instruction with a Cessna and Cirrus fleet, plus aircraft management and maintenance.
