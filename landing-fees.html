AUS Landing Fees

New general aviation landing fees at Austin-Bergstrom take effect October 1. A short, personal email to the people who decide can make a real difference — here's a starting point.

Why it matters

As written, the schedule is a flat $50 landing fee ($100 during F1, NASCAR, SXSW, and UT home-game weekends) on every aircraft up to 12,500 lbs — a Cessna 172 on a training flight is charged the same as a light jet. The ask below is narrow: exempt aircraft under 6,000 lbs, the primary and instrument trainer fleet, and keep the fee focused on the larger aircraft it was aimed at.

Make it yours — it works better

Council offices can spot a form letter immediately, and a message in your own words carries far more weight. Before you send, rewrite at least a few sentences. Some questions to help you think it through:

  • Do we want AUS charging a substantial fee to land?
  • Will this spread to other airports in the area?
  • Will you then be affected — as a student, renter, owner, instructor, or someone who flies in for a meal or a meetup?

Your own story (what you fly, what you'd pay, who you know who's training) is the strongest part. Feel free to cut anything that isn't true for you.
