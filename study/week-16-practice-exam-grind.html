Learning objectives

🔁 The protocol

There is no new material this week; the entire syllabus was covered by Week 15. The task now is repetition with real feedback: a full-length practice exam every other day, with the off-days spent entirely on reviewing 100% of the misses from the previous exam, understanding why the correct answer is correct, not just which letter it was.

DayDo
MondayFull-length practice exam
TuesdayReview every miss from Monday

ThursdayReview every miss from Wednesday

WeekendReview Friday's misses, then rest
The gate
Target three consecutive exams at 90% or better before you consider yourself ready to schedule the real test. The knowledge test itself needs 70% to pass; the extra margin is for the questions that ambush you on the day.
Practice exam tracker

Log each full-length exam score. Your target: three in a row at 90% or better. Scores stay in this browser only.

    📋 Administrative loose ends

    This is also the week to confirm your CFII endorsement is signed and dated (you will need it to schedule with PSI), and to double-check that your FAA Tracking Number (FTN) and IACRA account from Week 1 are both still valid and accessible.

    About the real test
    The Instrument Rating Airplane (IRA) knowledge test is 60 multiple-choice questions in 2.5 hours, with figures supplied in an FAA testing supplement. Practice with the same figures so reading a chart under time pressure is routine.
